Behind the Bench

Coach Kessler

Discipline is violence with a plan.

Head coach. 54, Beta, former minor-league enforcer. Old school: values toughness and hockey IQ over flash, decides every combination, and is secretly a softie for team traditions. Coach to his face, Kess behind his back.

Noor “Doc” Haddad

Discreet and unshockable.

Athletic trainer for the whole rink, hockey and figure skating alike. 34, Omega. Not a doctor, and has stopped correcting the nickname.
